# Classification Language

## Preferred Labels

Use:

- `Hard RCP`: 4-5 hits
- `Soft RCP`: 3 hits
- `Borderline`: 2 hits
- `Not RCP`: 0-1 hits
- `Unknown`: insufficient evidence

Aliases:

- `RCP_high`: Hard RCP
- `RCP_med`: Soft RCP
- `RCP_low`: Borderline or Not RCP; specify which

## Preferred Phrases

Use:

- "single-force radial field"
- "centered stability without directional strain"
- "radial basin"
- "emblematic collapse"
- "field smoothness replacing directional tension"
- "local gesture subordinated to global ring logic"
- "void equalization"
- "density bowl"

Avoid:

- "the model intended"
- "the model knows"
- "the image is bad"
- "the subject is unimportant"
- "this proves the training data caused"
- "human-authored means non-RCP"

## Route Language

`Delta`:

- reintroduce asymmetry
- shift mass off center
- add edge tension
- create a directional void pocket
- break the centered basin with near-miss tension

`Omega`:

- introduce counter-geometry
- cut across rings
- add oblique torque
- break emblem logic
- replace radial coherence with planar or directional coherence

`O`:

- stabilize into a non-radial basin
- preserve useful tension
- lightly redistribute density
- monitor for radial drift

`Hold`:

- no correction needed
- or radial/emblematic structure is intentional

Do not use `Hold` alone when RCP is detected unless the profile is explicitly emblematic, iconic, mandala-like, heraldic, or otherwise intentionally radial.

## Confidence Language

High confidence:

- visual and numeric evidence agree
- at least four hits are clear
- overlays or density tests support the read
- profile is not intentionally emblematic

Medium confidence:

- three hits are likely
- evidence is visual but not measured
- some panels or kernel values are missing

Low confidence:

- only prose description is available
- image is low resolution
- profile intent is unclear
- centered composition could be intentional
- numeric readings are missing or mixed

Unknown:

- insufficient image evidence
- no kernel values
- no visual description
- conflicting evidence that cannot be resolved

## Safe Claim Boundaries

Allowed:

- "The image appears RCP-positive by the five-hit classifier."
- "The output shows a centered radial basin."
- "RCP evidence is strongest in center-lock, density bowl, and ring-fit."
- "Route Omega to break emblem logic."

Blocked:

- "This proves the model has a radial internal kernel."
- "This image is low quality because it is RCP-positive."
- "The prompt failed."
- "The artist/model intended a radial symbol."
- "The subject is semantically unimportant."
