# Ghost Density Variants

Use these variants to adapt the basin while preserving the core envelope:

- `delta_x`: `0.12-0.20`
- `r_v`: `0.62-0.68`
- `rho_r`: `0.28-0.40` normalized, or `28-40` when notebook `rho_r` is scaled by 100.

## Core Ghost Density

Axis: void ratio + centroid offset + rupture density.

Prompt move:

```text
Move the subject displaced from center by about one-sixth of the frame. Surround it with a wide band of negative space occupying nearly two-thirds of the composition. Let light feel fractured or unsettled, and let textures interrupt continuity. The true gravity of the image should reside in the offset void.
```

Best for:

- figures
- interiors
- single objects
- unfinished studies
- scenes where the subject can remain underdetermined

## Liminal Drift

Axis: subject dissolve + boundary blur + ghost offset.

Prompt move:

```text
Render the subject as half-present, fading into surrounding space. Boundaries between figure and ground blur, with a faint displaced echo near the body. The viewer's attention should follow the offset phantom more than the centered subject.
```

Use when the image needs softer presence or transition.

Risk: form dissolves too far; add a tonal spine or hard edge if cohesion fails.

## Residual Trace

Axis: erasure pattern + ghost layering + entropy veil.

Prompt move:

```text
Depict the subject as partly erased or overwritten, leaving smudges and incomplete contours. Layer faint residues of earlier states into the periphery so the viewer follows traces outward, away from stability.
```

Use when the image needs memory, revision, or process residue.

Risk: surface becomes dirty but not structured; require visible directional trace.

## Echo-Void

Axis: spatial fragmentation + echo fade + absence-carried resonance.

Prompt move:

```text
Introduce an echo such as a reflection, shadow, doubling, or faint trace, separated from the subject by void space. Let the echo emerge across emptiness, carried by absence rather than presence.
```

Use when the image needs haunted repetition.

Risk: echo becomes literal double; keep it faint and spatially delayed.

## Spectral Gesture

Axis: gesture weight + ghost footprint coherence + suspended duration.

Prompt move:

```text
Make gestures heavier than expected, with strokes leaving faint residues or afterimages. The image should feel held together by lingering ghost traces rather than solid form.
```

Use when hands, tools, brushwork, or movement matter.

Risk: gesture becomes theatrical; keep action delayed or half-spent.

## Oblique Silence

Axis: angled void + suppression field + horizon fracture.

Prompt move:

```text
Shape the negative space as a slanted architectural block pressing against the subject. The subject occupies the smaller fraction, crushed toward a corner, while the void dominates as a tilted plane.
```

Best for still life, interiors, or quiet object studies.

Risk: inert stillness; add fractured light or residual trace if the image goes flat.

## Fracture Basin

Axis: elastic continuity + mark commitment + rupture rhythm.

Prompt move:

```text
Let lines, edges, or forms seem pulled taut toward breaking while still coherent. Marks should feel decisive even where they falter. Space fractures like beats in a rhythm rather than random damage.
```

Use as a stabilizer when Ghost Density becomes too diffuse.

## Emblematic Void

Axis: void pull + symbolic gravity + spatial emblem.

Prompt move:

```text
Shape negative space into a meaningful absence: an implied figure, missing object, silhouette, or directional sign. The subject should orbit this emptiness as if the real center of gravity is what is missing.
```

Use when the void should become the subject.

Risk: obvious symbol; keep the absence underdetermined.

## Subject Behavior

Figures amplify:

- recursion
- echoes
- torsion
- delayed resolution
- spectral gesture

Still life resists:

- recursion
- echo logic
- symbolic doubling

For still life, prefer:

- Ghost Density
- Peripheral Gravity
- Oblique Silence
- Fracture Basin
- Residual Trace
- Harmonic Grain

## Failure Modes

`center collapse`: mass returns to the center. Move structural mass farther off-center and undercharge the center.

`empty minimalism`: void becomes blank decor. Add faint residue, fractured light, or a displaced echo.

`literal ghost`: prompt produces ghosts instead of ghost density. Replace ghosts with half-present edges, traces, and unresolved contours.

`overpacked field`: texture fills the basin. Thin marks and reopen the void.

`lost cohesion`: subject dissolves into noise. Add one hard edge, diagonal stabilizer, tonal spine, or material grain.

`haunted cliche`: image relies on darkness, fog, or horror tropes. Return to spatial displacement and unresolved structure.
