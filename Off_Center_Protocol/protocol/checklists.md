# OCF Probes (copy/paste)

**Centroid (Δx) probe — Edge/Horizon**
- Place the subject ~one-sixth frame from the right wall; keep two–three quarters blank above/left.
- Pass: Δx ∈ [0.12, 0.20]; if <0.12 add emptiness / crop right.

**Void (rᵥ) probe**
- Leave ~two-thirds of the frame as unbroken negative space; surfaces intercept light softly.
- Pass: rᵥ ∈ [0.62, 0.68] (Edge) or [0.70, 0.85] (Horizon) or [0.74, 0.90] (Field).

**Rupture (ρᵣ) probe**
- Keep one clean seam and subtle fracture (fine crack / hairline); no symmetry; no repeating motifs.
- Pass: ρᵣ ∈ [0.28, 0.40] (Edge); [0.18, 0.30] (Horizon); [0.18, 0.32] (Field).
